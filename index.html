Christian Outreach & Restoration Ministry · Colo-i-Suva, Fiji

Reaching the lost with the hope of the Gospel

Seek to Save is a Christian outreach and restoration ministry led by Pastor Elia Betabi in Colo-i-Suva, Fiji. We meet people facing homelessness, exploitation, addiction and hardship with the love of Jesus Christ, and walk with them towards a new beginning.

“For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”Luke 19:10

What we hope to achieve

The Gospel changes lives, so that is where we begin

These are our hopes for the people we serve. They are hopes, not guarantees. Faith is always an invitation, and care is never conditional on it.

To be known and loved

The Gospel tells every person they are known and valued by God. We hope everyone who comes to us feels that in how they are welcomed and cared for.

Lives restored

We hope for healing, freedom from what has held people back, and the chance of a new beginning. Restoration is a journey, and we walk it alongside people.

Growing in faith

Through teaching, prayer and mentoring, we hope people come to know Jesus for themselves and find a community of believers who care for them.

A safe place called home

Safe beds, sanitation, shared meals and pastoral care under one roof, so that people have somewhere stable to heal and grow.

Hope that lasts

Through growing food and practical skills, we hope people can stand on their own feet and build a future with dignity.

The need

A shared room, a floor to sleep on

Today, people under Pastor Elia's care are accommodated in his home in Colo-i-Suva. With limited space and resources, residents currently sleep on the floor in one shared room.

Seek to Save hopes to create a dedicated facility with safe sleeping areas, sanitation, shared meals, pastoral care and space for learning and growth.

Our Story

Taking the Gospel beyond church walls

Seek to Save began with a desire to take the Gospel beyond church walls and meet people where they are. Pastor Elia Betabi and those serving alongside him seek to support people facing difficult circumstances with practical compassion and Christian discipleship.

The ministry's work includes care in the home, community outreach and growing food crops.

    Testimonies

    Lives being restored, in their own words

    Each testimony is shared by the person themselves, or with their informed consent, and told with dignity.

    How we protect people. Nothing is published until the person and the ministry have approved it in writing. Names may be shortened or changed, faces are not shown by default, and testimonies from children are not published.

    How a testimony is added

    1. Invitation
      The person is invited to share, and is free to say no.
    2. Written consent
      They agree in writing to the words, name and any photograph.
    3. Safety review
      The ministry checks that nothing could identify or endanger anyone.
    4. Publication
      The testimony is published with a date and can be withdrawn at any time.

    Policies

    How we protect people and gifts

    Draft commitments for review. Final wording will follow local legal advice.

    Privacy

    Donor and contact details are handled securely and used only for the purpose you give them. Consent is requested before updates are sent.

    Safeguarding

    We do not publish identifying information, locations, diagnoses or exploitation histories of vulnerable residents without lawful, informed approval. For children we apply especially protective standards and avoid identifiable stories by default.

    Donations & refunds

    Daily Care and Build a Home gifts are recorded separately. Refund terms and the handling of restricted gifts, including what happens if the building project changes, will be stated here before giving opens. We do not describe gifts as tax-deductible unless independently confirmed.

    Financial accountability

    We will name who manages donations, publish financial updates and report on the use of funds as milestones are approved.
